InboxOS

One calm inbox for Gmail and Outlook

InboxOS is an email client that brings your Gmail and Outlook accounts into a single inbox. You can read, write and organise email, and keep notes, tasks and reminders right next to it.

Unified inbox

Read, search, label, archive and reply across all your connected accounts in one place.

Snooze & reminders

Hide an email until you need it, or get reminded to follow up.

Notes & tasks

Attach private notes and to-dos to any conversation.

Schedule & undo send

Send later, or take a message back within a few seconds.

Subscriptions

See every newsletter you receive and unsubscribe in one click.

Team chat

Discuss emails with your workspace in channels and direct messages.

How InboxOS uses your Google data

When you connect a Gmail account, InboxOS asks Google for permission to read, send and organise your email (gmail.modify) and for your basic profile (name and email address). That access is used only to show your mailbox and carry out the actions you take in the app. InboxOS does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use your email to train AI models. You can disconnect at any time.
