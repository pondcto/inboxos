Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information InboxOS ("we", "the app") collects when you use it, how that information is used and protected, and the choices you have. InboxOS is an email client for Gmail and Microsoft Outlook accounts.

1. Information we access

  • Account profile. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft we receive your name, email address and, if available, your profile picture.
  • Email data. With your permission (Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, Microsoft permissions Mail.ReadWrite and Mail.Send) the app reads your messages, labels and drafts so it can display them. It also sends, archives, deletes, labels and otherwise organises email when you ask it to.
  • Content you create in InboxOS. Notes, tasks, reminders, snoozed conversations, scheduled messages, app settings, signatures and team chat messages.

2. How we use information

Information is used only to provide the features you use. That means showing your mailbox, searching it, sending and organising email on your instruction, delivering scheduled messages and reminders, and syncing your settings. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

InboxOS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. What we store and how it is protected

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and never shown to anyone.
  • Email bodies are fetched from Google or Microsoft when you open them and are not stored permanently. To keep the app fast, a short-lived cache of conversation metadata (sender, subject, date, labels and a short preview) is kept and cleared automatically.
  • Names and addresses of people you correspond with are stored to suggest recipients while you write.
  • Messages you schedule are stored encrypted until they are sent or cancelled.
  • Sign-in sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. Only a hash of each session token is stored.
  • Remote images in email are blocked by default so senders cannot track when you open a message.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent your information. Data leaves InboxOS only in these cases:

  • To Google or Microsoft, to perform the email actions you request.
  • To a newsletter sender, when you click Unsubscribe. The app then contacts the unsubscribe address that sender published.
  • To members of your InboxOS workspace, only when you choose to share an email in team chat. They see its subject, sender and a short preview.
  • When required by law.

No person reads your email unless you give explicit permission for a specific message, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or it is required by law.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • Disconnect an account at any time in Settings → Accounts. This deletes its stored tokens and cached data.
  • Revoke InboxOS's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or to your Microsoft account at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • To delete all of your InboxOS data, including notes, tasks and chat messages, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

InboxOS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
